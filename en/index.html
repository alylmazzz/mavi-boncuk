    How ordering works

      Tap an item to see all its photos. Pick the one that feels right.

          Frequently asked questions

          Size

          Write your child's age, height and weight. We measure the item in hand and tell you the size that will fit. If they're between sizes, we'll tell you that honestly too.

          Price

          Items sell quickly, and the same model can cost different amounts in different sizes. Rather than show a wrong price, we'd prefer to give you the current one when you ask.

          Shipping

          Some of our mums shop from far away. We tell you the shipping cost and time in a message, depending on your address.

          Exchanges

          Ask before you buy and we'll tell you clearly, depending on the item. For special-occasion pieces like party dresses, it's better for both of us to talk about this first.

          Hours

          We're open on Sundays too, from 12.00. If you write before you come, we'll check whether we have the size you want.

          Tell us what you're looking for

          Fill in the form and the message will open ready in WhatsApp. You can change it however you like before sending.
