    Frequently asked questions

    Should I buy the suit a size up?

    The jacket sleeve shouldn't go past the wrist, and the trouser hem should just touch the top of the shoe. A jacket one size up looks baggy in photos; it's better to buy their exact size.

    When should I look for a circumcision outfit?

    Once the date is set, look at least two weeks before. That leaves time to have the sleeves or trouser length altered by a tailor if needed.
