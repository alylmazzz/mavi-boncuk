    Frequently asked questions

    Can I buy a party dress without trying it on?

    If you're outside Ortaca, ask on WhatsApp with their height and weight and we'll measure the dress and tell you. If you're in Ortaca, you can come to the shop and try it on.

    When should I look for a graduation dress?

    Graduations are in June, so early May is a good time. That leaves time to settle the size and try shoes on.
